# EcomAI — WhatsApp & Facebook Channel Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-08-13 | **Source:** PROFILE.md + icp-definitions.md + dukaan-prosessed-gtm-plan.md
**Scope:** WhatsApp + Facebook go-to-market for EcomAI Phase 1 (India aggressive).
**Owner:** Founder (Amitav Panda) + AI agents (Content, Outreach, Lead-gen).

---

## 0. Objective

Execute the community + distribution arm of the GTM plan on WhatsApp and Facebook, per ICP. Target: **100 active community members across clusters in 30 days**, first paid customer from a community-referred lead in 60 days.

Channel split (from ICP definitions):
- **WhatsApp + Facebook → ICP 1 + 4** (wholesalers on Tally, offline-going-online)
- **Instagram (separate plan) + LinkedIn → ICP 2 + 3** (cloud kitchen/restaurant, D2C/ecom)
- Facebook **Page** is cross-ICP brand surface; Facebook **Groups** are ICP 1/4/3-heavy.

## 1. WhatsApp Plan

### 1.1 Infrastructure (3 levels, scale up as needed)

| Level | Tool | When | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. WhatsApp Business App** | Free app, one number | Today | Broadcasts to saved contacts only; no >256-person groups without platform |
| **2. WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API)** | Meta official API | When broadcasts >1000 / need automation | Opt-in only, template messages, full API |
| **3. EcomAI product WhatsApp engine** | Dogfood our own automation | Immediately for the 5 live businesses | Scheduled reminders, templates, campaigns — already built (PROFILE 4.7) |

**Decision:** Start Level 1 + dogfood Level 3 for existing businesses. Move to Cloud API when lead volume justifies (target: Week 6–8).

| Element | Detail |

**Guardrail:** one group per cluster at start. Split per-ICP (FMCG vs grocery vs D2C) only after each passes ~100 active members. Empty groups die.

## 3. Cross-Channel — Storefront Virality Loop (feeds both)

Every action on WhatsApp/FB points back to the shareable storefront:

```
Merchant shares yourstore.ecombharatai.com/store
        → buyer orders (no commission)
        → "Powered by EcomAI" on storefront
        → new merchant clicks → joins community / starts free trial
```

Rules:
- Every WhatsApp template + FB post ends with one CTA, always the storefront or community.
- Every community member is asked at close: "who else in your market needs this?" (referral loop, all ICPs).

## 6. Risks & Guardrails

- **Spam backlash:** opt-in only, never pitch in first DM, respect groups. One complaint = dead trust in ICP 1/4.
- **Empty groups:** never launch a group without 20+ seeded members. One cluster at a time.
- **Founder bandwidth:** everything routes through one inbox (Business Suite) + one WhatsApp number to protect founder time; agents do 80% of drafting.
- **Channel confusion:** WhatsApp = community + collections trust. FB = discovery + social proof. Don't force ads before organic proof exists.
- **ICP priority:** lead mix must follow scoring — ICP 1 > 2 > 3 > 4 — or WhatsApp/FB spend drifts to easy-but-low-value (ICP 4).
