## 4. Weekly Content Calendar (the attraction engine)

3-4 value posts per week, one per topic. These are the posts that make people STAY and invite others.

### 💰 Credit & Collections (Tue) — the money hook, your ICP 1/4 magnet
**Week 1:**
> **Your biggest collection mistake (and the fix)**
>
> Most businesses chase payments the same way: message → wait → message again. That's why 60+ day dues pile up.
>
> Fix: follow a **pattern**, not a feeling.
> 1. Day 0: send the invoice the moment goods are delivered
> 2. Day 15: gentle reminder — "just checking if the bill came through"
> 3. Day 30: clear ask — "can we settle X by this week?"
> 4. Day 45: offer split payment — any amount helps
> 5. Day 60: make it official — written notice, stop new credit
>
> Businesses that follow this recover 2× more than businesses that chase randomly. The secret isn't anger — it's **consistency**.
>
> **Which stage do most of your dues get stuck? Comment below.**
> *(soft EcomAI note — see section 6)*

### 🌐 Go Online (Wed) — the transformation hook, ICP 2/4 magnet
**Week 1:**
> **Your business can take online orders this week. Here's the 3-step path:**
>
> 1. **Choose the channel your customers already use.** For wholesalers: WhatsApp. For restaurants/D2C: Instagram + a storefront link.
> 2. **Make ordering easy.** One link your customers open → browse products → order → get invoice. No app install, no new habits.
> 3. **Automate the boring part.** Order confirmation, invoice, payment reminder — all automatic.
>
> The businesses doing this now are capturing the customers who stopped calling. Same product, better process = more sales.
>
> **What would you want automated first — orders, invoices, or reminders? Comment.**

### 🤝 Business Network (Thu) — the belonging hook, all ICPs
**Week 1:**
> **Every business owner needs 3 people in their network:**
> 1. A supplier who gives better terms
> 2. A buyer who orders regularly
> 3. A peer who has solved the problem you have today
>
> That's why this group exists. Post your intro + what you're looking for, and tag someone who could help.
> **Intro format:** Name | Business | City | I sell | I'm looking for
>
> Drop yours below 👇 Let's connect you.

### 🛠️ Daily Ops (Fri) — the savings hook, ICP 1/4 magnet

## 6. Soft EcomAI Promotion — the "value first" method

**Principle:** Every value post teaches a method. EcomAI appears only as *"the tool that does this automatically."* The reader feels helped, not sold. Then the demo closes.

### The soft-promo pattern (used at the end of value posts)
> **Want this on autopilot?** *(link)* This is what [EcomAI / ecombharatai.com] does automatically — 30-min free demo, your real data, no sales pitch.

### Per-topic soft angles

| Topic post teaches | EcomAI angle |
|---|---|
| Collection pattern (5-stage) | EcomAI schedules these reminders automatically, tracks who's overdue, scores credit risk |
| Go online 3-step | EcomAI launches the storefront + WhatsApp ordering in 5 days |
| Tally savings | EcomAI's AI dashboard reads your Tally data — credit, stock, collections visible Day 1 |
| Network | EcomAI's referral loop: "who else in your market needs this?" |
| Ideas & AI | EcomAI's AI daily summary, dead-stock alerts, demand insights |

### Frequency rule
- **2 soft mentions per week max.** Never two in a row.
- Soft mention = 1-2 lines + link, always after real value.
- Announcements group carries the *harder* offer (demos, trials) — 1×/week. Discussion groups stay pure value.
- **Never pitch in DMs from the community.** Answer → help → if they ask, demo.

### The 60-day path (soft → warm)
```
Week 1-2: Pure value posts. No product mention. Build trust.
Week 3: First soft mention (collections post → autopilot note).
Week 4-5: One announcement: "30-min free demo, your data, no pitch."
Week 6-8: Members who asked questions → private demo invites.
Result: community feels like a benefit, EcomAI is the answer they discovered.
```
