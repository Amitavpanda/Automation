# EcomAI — Ideal Client Profiles (ICP)

India-first. Target in priority order: **ICP 1 → ICP 2 → ICP 3 → ICP 4**. Same core product serves all four — messaging and funnel differ per ICP.

---

## ICP 1: FMCG / Grocery Wholesaler-Distributor on Tally (Anchor Client)

**Who:** Wholesaler or distributor owner/partner of an FMCG, grocery, or general-trade business selling to retailers. 1–20 staff. Multi-retailer credit book.

**Company profile:**
- 1–20 employees, single or multi-branch
- Currently on Tally (Tier A) or manual books (Tier B)
- Sells B2B to retailers with credit terms
- Recurring receivables problem

**Pain:**
- Money stuck in receivables past 90 days
- Manual ledger + chasing payments on WhatsApp
- Tally renewal cost (₹18K–54K/yr) with no AI, no storefront, no collections automation
- No visibility into which retailer is about to become a loss

**Trigger signals:**
- Uses Tally (visible in communities, forums, referral context)
- Posts/complains about unpaid dues, payment follow-ups
- Multi-branch or expanding retailer list
- CA-mandated Tally relationship (Path B) or free to move (Path A)

**Buyer persona:** Owner (35–55), trusts CA and referrals, not ads. Hindi/regional-first. Decision driver = collections + cost savings.

**Offer fit:**
- Tally import (free on-ramp) → AI dashboard + WhatsApp collections + storefront
- "You keep your CA and your Tally. We run your business and hand them a clean file."
- Both Path A (drop Tally, save renewal) and Path B (CA mandates Tally, EcomAI daily OS)

**Budget:** Priced below Tally renewal. Charge for AI layer, WhatsApp, storefront, multi-branch, auto-recon.

**Where to find:** IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Justdial, Google Maps wholesale markets, Tally user groups/forums, **CA referrals** (highest leverage), distributor networks.

## ICP 2: Cloud Kitchen / Restaurant / Food Delivery Business

**Who:** Cloud kitchen owner, restaurant group, QSR chain, food delivery operator. 1–50 outlets.

**Company profile:**
- Modern, WhatsApp-native, Instagram-active
- Multi-outlet or single busy outlet
- High order volume, delivery-app dependent

**Pain:**
- Order chaos across platforms, no unified storefront/app
- Manual inventory and stockouts
- Collections from delivery partners and corporate clients
- No branded direct-ordering channel (pays commissions to apps)

**Trigger signals:**
- Active on Instagram, posting about orders/scaling
- Multi-outlet expansion
- Food-tech or F&B community presence
- Complaints about commission/delivery apps

**Buyer persona:** Owner (25–40), WhatsApp-first, speed-driven. Decision driver = "live in 5 days."

**Offer fit:**
- Launch layer (branded site + app + storefront in 5 days)
- WhatsApp ordering + auto-reminders + inventory + AI insights
- Direct ordering channel to cut app commissions

**Budget:** Mid. Pays for speed. Launch sprint + monthly subscription.

**Where to find:** Instagram reels, F&B WhatsApp groups, restaurant/food-tech communities, food delivery ecosystem, LinkedIn.

## ICP 3: Ecommerce / Fashion / B2C D2C Brand (Scaling India)

**Who:** D2C/ecom/fashion brand founder or CMO with a live catalog, orders, and customer base. Growing India-first, global later.

**Company profile:**
- 2–50 employees
- Sells B2C online + WhatsApp
- Shopify/WordPress or marketplace seller (Amazon/Flipkart/Myntra)
- Scaling campaigns, seasonal spikes

**Pain:**
- Fragmented ops (catalog/orders/WhatsApp separate)
- No unified storefront, manual WhatsApp order handling
- No AI insights on stock, sales, demand
- Margin/pricing blind spots

**Trigger signals:**
- On Shopify/WordPress, marketplace-heavy
- Scaling ads/campaigns, seasonal spikes
- Instagram storefront, D2C founder groups
- Talking about automation, margins, AI

**Buyer persona:** Founder/CMO (25–45), growth-driven, English-first. Decision driver = automation + brand + AI.

**Offer fit:**
- Storefront + operations + WhatsApp + AI insights (risk, dead stock, opportunities)
- Shopify integration (roadmap) later
- Global wedge: same product, premium pricing

**Budget:** Highest. Growth budgets; global pricing possible.

**Where to find:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Shopify communities, D2C founder groups, ecom podcasts/newsletters.

## ICP 4: Offline Business Going Online (IndiaMART / Alibaba / Directory Sourced)

**Who:** Traditional offline business owner — manufacturer, exporter, supplier, trader, boutique, services business — who wants to go online but has no storefront/app/automation yet.

**Company profile:**
- Established offline business, 1–50 employees
- Sells via market, showroom, catalog, phone/WhatsApp, or marketplace presence
- Has listings on IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Alibaba, Justdial, Google Maps, classifieds
- No owned digital storefront, no ecommerce, no automation

**Pain:**
- Invisible online; competitors are on digital channels
- Manages orders manually via phone/WhatsApp/catalog
- No branded presence, no way to take online orders
- Doesn't know how/where to start; afraid of cost and complexity

**Trigger signals:**
- Listed on IndiaMART/TradeIndia/Alibaba/Justdial/Google Maps with phone contact
- Posting products but no owned website/app
- Active in trade/export communities
- "Want to go online" / digital-transformation intent

**Buyer persona:** Owner (30–55), often first-time digital buyer, price-sensitive, trust-driven. Decision driver = affordable "go online fast" without tech knowledge.

**Offer fit:**
- Launch layer only first: branded website + mobile app + storefront in 5 days
- Low-friction entry offer (lower entry price), then upsell ops + WhatsApp + AI
- Educational, hand-holding sales approach

**Budget:** Entry-level to start; upsell path to higher tiers. Anchor on "launch in 5 days" speed + affordability.

**Where to find:** IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Alibaba, Justdial, Google Maps, classifieds, trade/export directories, WhatsApp broadcast lists.

## Sourcing & Scoring Summary

| ICP | Primary channels | Score weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Wholesalers/Tally | IndiaMART, CA referrals, Tally groups, Maps | Highest |
| 2 — Food/Cloud kitchen | Instagram, F&B groups | High |
| 3 — D2C/Ecom | LinkedIn, Shopify, D2C groups | High |
| 4 — Going online | IndiaMART, Alibaba, Justdial, Maps | Medium |

Scoring priority when a lead matches multiple ICPs: **ICP 1 > ICP 2 > ICP 3 > ICP 4** (higher value + stronger moat story).

- **Reels-first content** targets ICP 2 + 3 (Instagram). **WhatsApp + Facebook + referrals** target ICP 1 + 4.
- **Real-data import demo** (send Tally backup → see your AI dashboard in 30 min) is the online closer for ICP 1 and 4.
- Referral loop applies to all: every close asks "who else in your market needs this?"
