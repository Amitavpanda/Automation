## Script

### Part 0 — Hook (0:00–0:30)

> "Every day, Indian wholesalers leave crores of rupees sitting in 'yesterday's credit' — money they sold, but haven't collected. Tally tells them the numbers. It never tells them who's about to default, which stock is dead, or what to do next. That's the gap EcomAI is built for."

### Part 1 — Why businesses need this (0:30–2:00)

> "India has lakhs of FMCG distributors, grocery wholesalers, cloud kitchens, and D2C brands. Most run on Tally + WhatsApp + a register book. Three problems every single one of them lives with:
>
> 1. **Stuck money** — receivables past 90 days, no system chasing it.
> 2. **No visibility** — they can't see today's risk, dead stock, or the retailer about to become a loss.
> 3. **No storefront** — retailers order by phone/WhatsApp, no branded ordering link, no app.
>
> Whoever fixes these three for the Indian wholesaler wins the market. That's exactly what EcomAI does — not as a feature, as the whole operating system."

**[On screen: 3 pain cards — Stuck Money / No Visibility / No Storefront]**

### Part 2 — What the product is (2:00–3:00)

> "EcomAI is an AI-powered commerce operating system for Indian wholesalers and businesses going online. Not a website builder. Not an app builder. An operating system that runs the business — five layers:
>
> - **Launch** — branded website + mobile app + storefront, live in under 5 days.
> - **Operations** — orders, inventory, warehouses, invoicing, manufacturers.
> - **Finance** — credit tracking, collections, ledgers, payment recording.
> - **Communication** — WhatsApp reminders, campaigns, message history.
> - **Intelligence** — AI daily briefing, credit risk scores, dead-stock detection, warehouse anomaly alerts.
>
> Every layer feeds the others. One system, one source of truth."

**[On screen: 5-layer stack diagram, layers light up one by one]**

### Part 3 — The market & competitors (3:00–4:00)

> "The competitive map is fragmented. CredFlow, Kapittx, Growfin do collections. Zoho Books does accounting. Shopify does storefronts. AgentCollect does AI collections — but for US enterprise, not Indian wholesalers. Nobody does the whole operating system for the Indian distributor.
>
> EcomAI's moat is the combination: **AI + WhatsApp collections + storefront + credit risk + Tally migration** — in one product, priced for India. And on the Tally front, we don't fight Tally — we make it invisible. Tally is the CA's tool; EcomAI is the daily business machine. We hand the CA a clean file in Tally's own format."
