# EcomAI — Video 2: Customer Explainer (Hindi + English)

**Audience:** Wholesalers, distributors, cloud kitchen owners, offline businesses going online
**Length target:** 4–6 min
**Tone:** Warm, practical, "aapki business ke liye", zero jargon. Aapko samjhao ki kya milega aur kaise.
**Language:** Hinglish (Hindi main, English terms). English version = same script translated clean.
**Key rule:** Customer video = "how it helps YOUR business every day", NOT features list. Start with storefront (what your retailers see), then dashboard (what you see).

## Customer video key rules

1. **Storefront pehle** — retailers ka experience dikhao (customer video ka sabse zyada asar)
2. **Dashboard baad mein** — business owner ko apna faayda dikho
3. **Numbers Hinglish mein** — ₹14.6 crore, 200 retailers, 5 din — concrete rehna
4. **CTA = WhatsApp**, email nahi — Indian SMB WhatsApp pe convert karta hai
5. **Mobile app teaser** — 10 sec, "phone se poora business" — excitement + future promise
