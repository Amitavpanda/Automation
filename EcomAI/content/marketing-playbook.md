# EcomAI — Marketing Playbook: Sell Outcomes, Not Software

**Source:** Postiz founder (Nevo David, @wickedguro) crossed $2M ARR — "Stop selling software, start selling results." https://x.com/wickedguro/status/2086788813301661865
**Purpose:** guiding principles for ALL EcomAI reels, posts, articles, outreach. Read this before drafting any content.

---

## The Core Rule

> **Never sell the solution. Sell the result.**

Everyone can vibe-code an app now → software alone doesn't sell. Distribution is the moat. When we write about EcomAI, we don't write "a tool that gives you a storefront." We write *what the business gains.*

### The frame for EcomAI

| ❌ Don't sell the solution | ✅ Sell the result |
|---|---|
| "A platform with storefront, books, WhatsApp, AI" | "Your business stops fighting its own operations and goes back to marketing and sales" |
| "Credit risk scoring feature" | "Never ship to an overdue account again — stop losses you never saw coming" |
| "Smart reconciliation" | "Hours your staff spent re-typing sales into the books, back in your pocket" |
| "5 vendors in one system" | "Your competitor runs one system. You run five vendors, five logins, five bills" |
| "WhatsApp automation" | "The money stuck in unpaid invoices comes back, automatically" |
| "Built for wholesalers, D2C, agencies" | "Whoever you are — if you sell, EcomAI runs the whole pipeline" |

**Test for every piece:** does the copy lead with a business outcome (money, time, growth) or a feature list? If features → rewrite.

## 2026 Channel Rules (what works, what's dead)

### Deprioritize / don't bet on
- **Ads** — CAC math broken for cheap subs. Skip at launch.
- **Traditional SEO** — "traffic went up, revenue didn't. People ask ChatGPT now."
- **Company-update social** ("we shipped X!") — dead channel.
- **Building-in-public alone** — views don't convert. Use it for authority, not conversion.

### Put money here
1. **Authority** — post ONE proof metric consistently, with verifiable proof. People believe you once you show receipts; then everything converts better.
   - **EcomAI proof metrics:** live businesses (4 → 10 → 25), Founding-100 spots claimed (12/100), Tally imports done, collections recovered, app-store launch. Public live dashboard = trust bomb.
2. **X Articles** — biggest current growth hack. X favors **saves + read-time** over likes/comments.
   - Tricks: quote-repost your article with ~30s video (they amplify each other); repurpose everything (tweet/video → article); collaborate with customers who got results.
3. **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** — be cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude. Much easier than SEO — content can live anywhere (Reddit, LinkedIn articles, X articles, GitHub awesome-lists), not just your site.
   - **KEY: win a narrow niche.** Compete as *"AI OS for Indian wholesalers to go online"* — NOT *"e-commerce platform"* (would lose to Shopify). Win queries like "how to leave Tally", "best platform for wholesale distribution India".
   - Plant REAL answers in Reddit (r/IndiaBusiness, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur) — not LLM slop. One good comment sells forever.
4. **Tutorials** — content close to the product. "How a Delhi eyewear wholesaler put 250 partners online." "How to stop shipping to overdue accounts."
5. **Lead magnets** — keyword tricks banned on X; use Instagram. ONE source of truth (one big video/article) + every magnet points to it.
6. **UGC / AI UGC** — money game until one viral video, then repeat non-stop. Later stage.

## EcomAI Content Archetypes (map every piece to one of these)

### Type A — Outcome Story (the conversion piece)
Real business, real before/after. "Wholesaler ran 5 vendors → runs 1 system. Here's what changed." Close to product, close to money.
- 60s video / X article / LinkedIn narrative.

### Type B — The Frame Reel (the viral hook)
The "5 vendors vs 1 system" punch. 15–30s. Bold, sharp, money-aware. Designed for saves + shares, not likes.
- Hooks: *"Your competitor runs one system. You run five vendors, five logins, five bills — and an evening re-typing sales into the books."* / *"Every invoice shipped to an overdue account is a loss you chose."*

### Type C — AI-on-top insight (authority + shareability)
AI sits on top of the business → owner goes back to marketing and sales. Educational, quotable, ends with a specific question.
- "What would you automate first?" / "What eats most of your week?"

### Type D — Proof / Authority (trust)
Post real metrics: live businesses, Founding-100 progress, app-store launch, Tally imports. Consistent cadence. Every claim verifiable.

### Type E — AEO answer (LLM citation mining)
Real, valuable answers planted on Reddit/LinkedIn/X where business owners ask "how do I go online / leave Tally / manage wholesale credit."

## Video Length Playbook
- **15s** — Frame Reel (Type B): the 5-vendors hook.
- **30s** — Main launch video: walkthrough cut, screen-focused.
- **60s** — Outcome Story (Type A): founder + customer narrative.
- **90s+** — Tutorial / AEO (Type E): "how a wholesaler goes online."

## Non-Negotiables
- **No fake metrics.** Specific TRUE numbers beat invented ones. If a beta user reports a real before/after ("follow-up time from 2 days to 2 hours"), that's viral gold — use it.
- **No hype fluff.** No "revolutionary," "game-changing," "seamless."
- **Hindi, English, Hinglish** — broader reach is the point.
- **Money-aware framing** for viral content; warm + confident for brand content.
- **Result first, features second. Always.**
