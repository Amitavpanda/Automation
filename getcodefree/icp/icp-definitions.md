# GetCodeFree — Ideal Client Profiles (ICP)

## ICP 1: US Seed/Series A SaaS Founder

**Who:** Technical or semi-technical founder of a B2B SaaS startup
**Company size:** 2–20 people
**Funding:** Pre-seed to Series A ($500k–$5M raised)
**Pain:** Needs to ship fast but can't afford a full in-house team yet
**Trigger signals:**
- Recently raised a round
- Posted "we're hiring" on LinkedIn but can't afford FTEs
- Talking about AI features they want to build
- MVP exists but needs scaling
**Offer fit:** MVP in 5 weeks → Managed partner
**Budget:** $15k–$60k for project, $3k–$10k/month retainer
**Where to find:** LinkedIn, YC companies list, ProductHunt launches, HN Who is hiring

---

## ICP 2: US/EU CPO or CTO at Growth-Stage Startup

**Who:** Product or engineering leader at a 20–100 person startup
**Company size:** 20–100 people
**Funding:** Series A–B
**Pain:** Engineering team is overloaded; needs a trusted external team for a defined workstream
**Trigger signals:**
- Job postings for senior engineers or AI engineers
- Product announcements with no engineering bandwidth to ship
- Talking about "moving faster" on social
**Offer fit:** AI automation sprint, MVP to production, Managed partner
**Budget:** $20k–$100k for project, $5k–$15k/month retainer
**Where to find:** LinkedIn, AngelList, conference speaker lists

---

## ICP 3: Dubai / Middle East Business Owner

**Who:** Owner or ops director of an established business wanting AI/digital transformation
**Company size:** 20–200 people
**Industry:** Logistics, retail, real estate, finance, services
**Pain:** Manual ops, no tech team internally, wants automation
**Trigger signals:**
- Attending AI/tech events in Dubai
- Posting about digital transformation
- Looking for vendors on LinkedIn
**Offer fit:** AI automation sprint, internal tools, dashboards
**Budget:** $10k–$50k for project, $2k–$8k/month retainer
**Where to find:** LinkedIn Dubai, Gulf Business, startup communities

---

## ICP 4: Indian Startup Founder (Fast Cash)

**Who:** India-based early-stage founder who needs fast, high-quality build
**Company size:** 1–10 people
**Pain:** Needs a trusted local agency that understands Indian startup context
**Trigger signals:**
- In startup communities (iSPIRT, NASSCOM, local VC ecosystems)
- Raising first round and needs product to show investors
- Referral from network
**Offer fit:** MVP in 5 weeks
**Budget:** ₹3L–₹8L for project
**Where to find:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X India tech community, startup Slack groups, direct referrals
