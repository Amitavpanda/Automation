# GetCodeFree — ICP Master Sheet

## ICP-A1: US Seed/Series A SaaS Founder

**Role:** CEO / Founder / Co-Founder
**Stage:** Seed to Series A ($250k–$5M raised)
**Size:** 2–20 people
**Industry:** B2B SaaS, dev tools, AI products, fintech, HR tech
**Geography:** US (primary), EU (secondary)
**Budget:** $10k–$60k project, $3k–$10k/month retainer

**Top Pains:**
- "We need to ship faster but can't afford to hire 3 senior engineers"
- "Our MVP is held together with duct tape and we're about to demo to investors"
- "We want to add AI features but no one on the team has built with LLMs before"

**Trigger Events:**
- Just announced a funding round (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, TechCrunch)
- Posting "we're hiring senior engineers" but position open 30+ days
- Launched on Product Hunt in last 90 days
- Posting about shipping speed or technical debt publicly
- YC batch announcement

**Where to Find:**
- YC companies list (ycombinator.com/companies)
- Product Hunt — recent launches
- LinkedIn — search "founder" + "raised" + specific stack keywords
- HN Who is Hiring threads
- AngelList / Wellfound

**Language They Use:**
- "move fast", "ship fast", "velocity", "technical debt", "we're a small team"
- "we need to build X but don't have the bandwidth"
- "looking for a strong engineering partner"
- "AI-first product", "LLM integration", "agent workflow"

**Offer Fit:** MVP in 5 weeks → Managed partner retainer
**Disqualifiers:** Pre-idea, no funding clarity, wants hourly billing only
**Strategic Value:** HIGH — referral network, US ticket size, long LTV
**Ease of Close:** MEDIUM — 45–90 day cycle
**Priority Tier:** A

## ICP-A2: US/EU CPO or CTO at Growth-Stage Startup

**Role:** CPO / CTO / VP Engineering / Head of Product
**Stage:** Series A–B
**Size:** 20–100 people
**Industry:** SaaS, fintech, e-commerce tech, healthtech, HR tech
**Geography:** US (primary), EU (secondary)
**Budget:** $20k–$100k project, $5k–$15k/month retainer

**Top Pains:**
- "Our internal team is maxed out — we need a trusted external team for this workstream"
- "We want to ship an AI feature in Q3 but our engineers aren't AI-native"
- "Our codebase needs serious refactoring before we can scale"

**Trigger Events:**
- Engineering job posts open for 60+ days (signals capacity problem)
- Product roadmap announcements with no engineering headcount to match
- Series B announced — now needs to scale fast
- CTO/CPO posting about "engineering velocity" or "technical debt" on LinkedIn

**Where to Find:**
- LinkedIn — filter by title, company size, industry, funding stage
- Conference speaker lists (SaaStr, ProductCon, AI Engineer Summit)
- AngelList / Wellfound company pages
- Crunchbase — filter by funding date

**Language They Use:**
- "bandwidth", "capacity", "execution speed", "trusted partner"
- "AI-native engineering", "LLM pipeline", "production-grade AI"
- "we need to move on this fast", "we don't have the in-house expertise"

**Offer Fit:** AI automation sprint / MVP to production / Managed partner
**Disqualifiers:** Wants full team on-site, needs agency to manage their existing engineers
**Strategic Value:** HIGH — large contracts, multi-year potential
**Ease of Close:** MEDIUM — 30–60 day cycle with clear budget authority
**Priority Tier:** A

## ICP-B1: Dubai / Middle East Business Owner

**Role:** CEO / MD / Operations Director / Founder
**Stage:** Established SME or growth business
**Size:** 20–200 people
**Industry:** Logistics, real estate, retail, finance, professional services
**Geography:** UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar
**Budget:** $10k–$50k project, $2k–$8k/month retainer

**Top Pains:**
- "We're doing everything manually — we need to automate our operations"
- "We want to build a customer-facing app but don't have tech capabilities"
- "We've heard about AI but don't know where to start"

**Trigger Events:**
- Attending AI/tech events in Dubai (GITEX, Step Conference)
- Posting about digital transformation
- Hiring for "IT Manager" or "Digital Transformation" role
- Opening new branch or market that needs tech support

**Where to Find:**
- LinkedIn Dubai — filter by title + industry
- GITEX attendee lists
- Gulf Business magazine features
- Dubai Chamber of Commerce events
- WhatsApp business communities (warm intro needed)

**Language They Use:**
- "automate", "digitize", "streamline", "reduce manual work"
- "we need a system for", "our team wastes time on"
- "fast delivery", "reliable vendor", "proven solution"

**Offer Fit:** AI automation sprint, internal tools, dashboards
**Disqualifiers:** Wants fully on-site team, government procurement process only
**Strategic Value:** MEDIUM — good ticket, fast cash, network referrals
**Ease of Close:** MEDIUM — 30–45 day cycle, relationship-driven
**Priority Tier:** B

## ICP-B2: Indian VC-Backed Startup Founder

**Role:** CEO / Founder / Co-Founder
**Stage:** Pre-seed to Seed (Indian VCs: Blume, Elevation, Accel India)
**Size:** 2–15 people
**Industry:** Fintech, agritech, edtech, B2B SaaS, D2C
**Geography:** Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad
**Budget:** ₹3L–₹10L project, ₹75k–₹2L/month retainer

**Top Pains:**
- "We need to build fast before our funding runway runs out"
- "We need a technical co-founder but can't find one — can you be our team?"
- "We need to show investors a working product in 6 weeks"

**Trigger Events:**
- Just closed a round (YourStory, Inc42 coverage)
- Posting about product development urgency on LinkedIn
- Pitch competition participation
- Referral from common connection

**Where to Find:**
- Inc42, YourStory funding news
- LinkedIn India startup community
- iSPIRT, NASSCOM events
- Local startup meetups in Bengaluru
- Direct referral network

**Language They Use:**
- "MVP", "investor demo", "runway", "move fast"
- "need a good tech team", "can't afford to hire FTEs yet"
- "want to build in India with global quality"

**Offer Fit:** MVP in 5 weeks
**Disqualifiers:** No clear funding, vague problem statement, wants equity-only deal
**Strategic Value:** MEDIUM — lower ticket but fast close and strong referral network
**Ease of Close:** HIGH — 15–30 day cycle
**Priority Tier:** B

## ICP-C1: Indian Bootstrapped Founder (Fast Cash)

**Role:** Solo founder / bootstrapped CEO
**Stage:** Pre-revenue to early revenue
**Size:** 1–5 people
**Industry:** Any consumer or B2B idea
**Geography:** India
**Budget:** ₹1.5L–₹4L project

**Offer Fit:** Basic MVP build
**Priority Tier:** C — lower priority, take only when capacity allows
**Note:** Good for referrals and testimonials if delivered well
