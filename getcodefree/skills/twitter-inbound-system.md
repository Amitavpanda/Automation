# Twitter Inbound Lead System for GetCodeFree

Two modes: **auto scrape** or **manual input**. Agent drafts replies & posts. You review before posting.

## Skills

| # | Skill | Auto Mode | Manual Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Top 10 Tweets | Scrape feeds → return 10 best by views+checklist | You pass URLs |
| 2 | Best Article | Scrape feeds → find 1 best article | You pass article URL |
| 3 | Draft Replies | Draft replies to Skill 1 tweets | You pass reply URLs |
| 4 | Draft Posts | Draft posts from scrape insights | You pass topic ideas |
| 5 | LinkedIn Cross-Post | Draft LinkedIn from best content today | Same |

## Daily Workflow (10 AM)

1. **Skill 1** — Scrape top 10 tweets OR you found some. Review.
2. **Skill 2** — Scrape best article OR you found one. Read.
3. **Skill 3** — Draft replies to selected tweets (from scrape or your picks). Review + post.
4. **Skill 4** — Draft posts (from insights or your topics). Review + post.
5. **Skill 5** — Cross-post best to LinkedIn. Review + post.
