# GetCodeFree — Primary Goal

## Mission — Build a Company, Leave Full-Time Job

**Current**: 1 lakh INR/mo job (full-time). Also building EcomAI + GetCodeFree.
**Target**: $4k MRR (~4.6 lakhs INR/mo incl. 50-60k expenses) in **3-4 months** → grow GetCodeFree into a real dev agency/company.
**Fallback**: Remote job 4-5 lakhs/mo for 4-5 hrs/day while scaling agency (Toptal, Upwork).
**End state**: Founder running GetCodeFree agency + EcomAI. Independence. Not a freelancer — an agency that can hire.

## North Star: Platforms Feed the Agency

Platforms are NOT just cash-flow fallback. They are **client acquisition channels for the agency** (the playbook that works — proven by peers):

1. **Win client on platform** (Upwork/Arc/Guru/Toptal) with agency positioning, not freelancer positioning
2. **Deliver, over-deliver** → client trusts the studio
3. **Convert to agency account** — retainer (direct or on-platform), upsell Offers 1-5
4. **Grow** — sub-teams, expanded scope, case studies from real revenue work
5. Platform fees = lead-gen cost. $1-2k fees to land a ₹8-25L client = great ROI

## Strategy

1. **Agency revenue** — MVP sprints ($5-15k each), AI automations ($2-8k), retainers ($1-3k/mo), Enterprise .NET/ABP builds (₹6-20L)
2. **Platform funnel** — Upwork, Toptal, Arc, Guru, Contra, Lemon/Flexiple, Reddit/HN/Indie Hackers = client acquisition, not just income
3. **ABP/DDD wedge** — rare niche (~3-4 ABP specialists worldwide). Profiles positioned as "AI-native .NET agency + ABP/DDD specialist". ABP jobs → premium wins (low competition); DDD/.NET jobs → volume hours
4. **Direct outreach** — X/LinkedIn DMs to funded founders; agency overflow (Inwizards, nopStation, Nile Bits) → no-bid contracts
5. **Competitor differentiation** — AI-native delivery, senior-only, founder-led, ABP/DDD mastery
6. **Side income bridge** — DataAnnotation.tech coding track ($40-75/hr) while agency ramps

## Scoring Rule (drives agents)

Every scraped lead scored by agency potential, not just win probability:
- **Retainer-shaped client** (SaaS owner, ongoing product, funded startup, ABP/ASP.NET Zero production shop) → agency candidate, HIGH priority, pitch as studio
- **One-off small gig** → cash-flow only, LOW priority
- **Fits Offers 1-5** → qualify, upsell to retainer
