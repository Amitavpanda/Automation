# GetCodeFree — Growth Strategy

## The Problem

Full-time job + EcomAI + agency = limited bandwidth (<2 hrs/day for agency).
Need to hit $4k MRR (~4.6L INR/mo) in 3-4 months to quit job.

## The Solution

**Bottleneck is pipeline, not delivery.** I can build anything — full-stack, mobile, AI, infra — 8+ years senior experience. Once client is onboarded, I can deliver solo or expand team.

But as CEO, sales/marketing is the gating factor. That's where focus goes now. Post-delivery team expansion can follow once revenue justifies it.

## Strategy Pillars

### 1. Founder-Led Outbound (Highest Leverage)

Target ICP-A1, A2, B1 with personalized DMs.
- **X/Twitter**: Founders building in public, posting about dev struggles
- **LinkedIn**: CTOs/CPOs with capacity signals (hiring posts, burnout signals)
- **Automated**: browser-use agents scrape + score + queue for my review

### 2. Founder-Led Content (Medium-Long Term)

Build personal brand on LinkedIn/X as the senior engineer who ships.
- Post 3-4x/week about real builds, lessons, takes on AI
- Content angles from InboundSystem Space 04
- Direct CTA: "DM me if you're building something"

### 3. Freelance Platforms (Short-Term Cash)

Upwork, Toptal for contracts $4-5k/mo while scaling.
- Target: 1-2 concurrent contracts
- Use to supplement until agency MRR covers full target

### 4. Inbound Capture

Clear CTA on website + social → booked strategy calls.
Qualify fast using ICP scoring → proposal → close.

## Operating Cadence

| Activity | Frequency | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Scrape leads (automated) | Daily | 10-20 leads/day |
| Review + DM leads | Daily (30 min) | 5 personalized DMs |
| Post content | 3-4x/week | Build authority |
| Apply Upwork/Toptal | Weekly | 5-10 proposals |
| Strategy calls | As booked | Close 2/mo |

## Fallback

If agency MRR lags, take remote job 4-5L/mo (4-5 hrs/day).
Continue scaling agency + EcomAI in parallel.
