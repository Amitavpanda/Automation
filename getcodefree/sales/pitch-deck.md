# GetCodeFree Sales Pitch

## Elevator Pitch

> GetCodeFree is an AI-native product studio. We ship mobile apps, web platforms, and AI automations for startups and growth-stage businesses — with a lean senior team, production-grade execution, and founder-level speed. Ship your MVP in ~3 weeks.

## Target ICP

- **Founders** with validated idea, need technical co-founder / build team
- **Startups** with rough MVP that needs production hardening
- **SMBs** wanting AI automation (lead gen, content, CRM, internal tools)
- **Agencies** needing overflow capacity for mobile/web builds

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Compare to hiring in-house (salary + benefits + time). We ship in weeks, not months. |
| "Can't trust remote" | Senior-only team, founder-led. Weekly calls, Slack/WhatsApp async. |
| "What about post-launch?" | Monthly support retainers. Bug fix SLA under 24h. |
| "Why not freelancers?" | Single accountable team. Not managing 5 disparate freelancers. |

## Pitch Angles by Segment

| Segment | Angle |
|---|---|
| Early-stage founder | "Ship MVP in 3 weeks for investor demos" |
| Post-MVP startup | "Production-harden your MVP before scaling" |
| SMB | "Automate your ops in 5 days with AI" |
| Agency partner | "White-label mobile/web builds" |
