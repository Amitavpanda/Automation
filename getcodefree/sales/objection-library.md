# GetCodeFree — Objection Library

## Objection 1: "You're based in India — will quality be an issue?"

**Real concern:** Past bad experience with offshore agencies
**Response angle:** 
- Amitav is a founding engineer at a US AI SaaS startup — same quality bar as a US hire
- We don't do body-shopping — we own the output
- NativeNest is on the App Store. AccounSaathi runs in production. The work speaks.
**Proof to use:** NativeNest, US startup engagement

---

## Objection 2: "We tried an agency before and it didn't work out"

**Real concern:** Accountability, communication, and output ownership
**Response angle:**
- Most agencies fail because they're staffing firms pretending to be product teams
- We work like a founding engineering team — we care about the outcome, not just billing hours
- We start with a small defined sprint so you can verify quality before committing
**Proof to use:** Any completed project + clear scope offer

---

## Objection 3: "Your team seems small — can you handle our project?"

**Real concern:** Capacity and reliability
**Response angle:**
- We're intentionally lean and AI-augmented — 3x throughput per engineer
- We don't overcommit. We take fewer projects and deliver completely.
- Ask us about our current capacity honestly — we'll tell you if we're the right fit
**Proof to use:** AI-native workflow positioning

---

## Objection 4: "We don't have budget for this right now"

**Real concern:** Either genuinely no budget, or not convinced of ROI yet
**Response angle:**
- If genuinely no budget: "When do you expect to have budget? Let's stay in touch."
- If ROI concern: "What would it cost you to hire a senior engineer for 3 months vs. getting this shipped in 5 weeks?"
- Offer a smaller entry point: AI automation sprint at lower price point
**Proof to use:** Speed and ROI framing

---

## Objection 5: "We're still exploring options"

**Real concern:** Not ready to decide, comparing vendors
**Response angle:**
- "What would make this a clear yes for you?"
- Give them a specific, time-boxed offer: "We have capacity for one project starting in 2 weeks — after that we're booked."
- Send a relevant case study and follow up in 5 days
**Proof to use:** Urgency + capacity scarcity (real, not fake)

---

## Objection 6: "Can you do a small test project first?"

**Real concern:** Want to verify quality before committing
**Response angle:**
- YES — this is exactly how we recommend starting
- Propose a 2-week paid discovery/sprint at ₹50k–₹1L
- This removes all risk for them and proves quality fast
**Proof to use:** This is a buying signal — treat it as a close
